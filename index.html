The campus, at a glance

Small enough to learn in a week.

Sport runs along the west edge: the track first, then the badminton and basketball courts. Teaching Buildings A and B sit at the top of campus, with the Training Building and Student Center to their east. The student dormitories line the eastern edge, and below the Student Center the teacher dormitories and the Lakeside Restaurant sit around a small lake.

Click a building

Every shape on the map opens a short description, tells you where it sits, and shows a photo of it.
