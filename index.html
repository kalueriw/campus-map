The campus, at a glance

Small enough to learn in a week.

Sport runs along the west edge: the track first, then the basketball courts. Teaching Buildings A and B sit at the top of campus, with the Training Building and Student Center to their east. The student and teacher dormitories line the eastern edge, and the lake and Teacher Center sit in the south-east.
